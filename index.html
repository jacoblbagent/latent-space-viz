Latent Space

— hidden states, mean-pooled per sentence, drawn in 3D.

Field guide

Every dot is one real sentence pushed through a real model. What the picture is, how it was built, and where it stops being trustworthy.

01 · fundamentals

1What a hidden state is›

The model never sees words. A sentence is split into tokens, and each of the 24 transformer layers rewrites a vector for every one of them. Averaging the token vectors at the end of a layer leaves a single vector of 896 numbers — that sentence's mean-pooled hidden state at that depth.

One dot is one sentence at one layer. Nothing here is simulated: the geometry is the model's own, flattened to three axes so it fits on a screen.

2What the dots and colours mean›

416 hand-written sentences across 13 domains, 32 per domain. Colour is the domain label, applied by the author and never shown to the model — so a domain landing together means the model arranged those sentences similarly on its own.

Hover a point to read the sentence and how many of its five nearest neighbours share its domain in the view you are looking at. Click a domain in the legend to isolate it; click again to restore all.

02 · how it is built

3Depth: reading the layer slider›

Layer 0 is the embedding table; layer 24 is the last transformer block — 25 tensors in all. The same sentence is a different point at every depth, and that is the subject of the piece:

0–2
Tiny and near-isotropic. Directions are not yet organised by meaning.
3–21
Variance collapses into a few “rogue” directions — the three PCA axes hold ~94% of it — and topical purity plateaus around 45–60%.
22–23
Purity jumps to ~92% and, in the neighbourhood layout, thirteen tight islands appear.
24
The norm explodes to roughly 1000×. That is the signature of the huge activations feeding the output head.

Arrow keys step one layer; space plays through all of them.

4Two layouts, and what a distance means›

Both layouts draw the same activations. They make different promises.

PCA
One shared linear basis for every layer, so the slider moves the model inside a fixed frame. Distances and cluster gaps are meaningful. Cost: at layer 23 it shows only 40% separation — the semantic structure is not three-dimensional, and a linear view squashes it into a cigar.
UMAP
Fitted per layer so semantic neighbours land together: ~90% separation at layer 23. Cost: distances are not metric, gap width is approximate, and two layers cannot be compared position by position.
Why these UMAP settings?›

100 neighbours, min_dist=0.6, picked by measuring gap inflation rather than by eye. The first attempt (15, 0.12) drew moats 3.47× wider than the full 896-d space supports; this pair holds inflation near 1.17× while keeping separation near 92%. Fewer sentences per domain inflates the gaps further, which is why the corpus is 32 per domain rather than 8.

03 · reading the numbers

5The four readouts›
Separation
Share of each sentence's five nearest neighbours that share its domain, measured inside the view. Compare it with the full-space figure in the same caption. High means the structure survived the projection.
Spread
Mean squared norm of the centred state, relative to the widest layer. It grows about 1000× from layer 0 to 24.
Variance
PCA only: how much of the layer's variance the plotted axes capture. Neighbourhood layouts report “not metric” instead, because they do not have axes.
Gap inflation
How much wider the gaps read here than in the full 896-d space. 1.00× is faithful; 3.00× is a moat the data does not have; below 1.00× means domains are being collapsed onto each other, which is what the linear view does (0.40× at layer 22).

The bar strip underneath is full-space separation for all 25 layers. Click any bar to jump to that depth.

6Scale, and the generation path›

Normalised fits each layer into a fixed cube so every depth is legible. True scale uses the real per-layer norms, so layer 24 dominates — the shapes differ between the two modes, and that difference is itself the measurement.

Generation path asks the model to write about the ocean, embeds each greedy token step, and draws the trajectory through the final layer's PCA frame. It stays in one neighbourhood instead of wandering. It is only valid on the final layer with PCA axes, which is why the toggle switches both.

04 · limits

7What this cannot tell you›
  • 416 sentences is a small sample. The layer profiles are indicative, not a benchmark.
  • Mean pooling is one summary of a layer. Token-level geometry — how an individual word moves — is not shown.
  • UMAP layouts are stochastic and fitted independently per layer, so positions only compare within a single layer.
  • Clustering is not comprehension: a domain holding together does not mean the model has a concept of that domain, only that it represents those sentences similarly.
